<h1> Content </h1>
Since my first blog entry, I have researched different technologies that's artists can use for art and drawing, in many ways to be creative with these tools, and even when being a beginner you still have the many chances to start at a great position. I started by researching on google for "Art apps that artists can use" which helped me discover programs such as <a href=https://krita.org/es/> Krita digital painting </a>, <a href=https://www.escapemotions.com/products/rebelle/about> Rebelle real media paint software</a>, <a href=https://www.pureref.com> PurRef</a>. I learned that Krita provides digital drawing tools, Rebelle stimulates traditional painting materials such as watercolor, and PureRef helps artists organize reference images. Then I searched on software, I wanted to discover physical devices that's artists use or maybe some that not much people know about. I searched "Art devices that artists use." This helped me find inventions such as the <a href= https://www.xencelabs.com/us?srsltid=AYCX9vIJpRI7MtK4LRsPU3Kf_D3Rucv94AnSR2Y0LNeyTgvJUFGi2gS1> Xencelabs</a>, <a href=https://veikk.com/es?srsltid=AYCX9vKQNTzB28tQCdXICU3QNi3ehh8i_chNnj8gqOJxNrpUBuAx-rQ2> Veikk VK1060 pro drawing tablet</a>, <a href=https://www.xp-pen.com> XPPen drawing glove</a>, and <a href=https://www.tourboxtech.com/en/?srsltid=AYCX9vJcQdYQtIqIpMn15iokYWMaALoy1EX8Ks6zNanAH50AccqKs1Vl> TourBox lite </a>. These devices provided different features that help artists access shortcuts, control their digital drawings, and make the drawing process more comfortable.

<h1> Organization </h1>
As I gathered more information, I wanted to find a way to organize all the technologies I discovered. I first had organized all of this into software and hardware, but there are many other ways to organize these inventions, not just one. My decision was to organize everything into three categories, which I based on their purposes. These three categories were artistic preparation, creative assistance, and digital creation. I decided on choosing these three categories because I wanted to show how each invention supports artists in different ways, whether it helps them prepare their artwork, makes the drawing process easier, or allows them to create many pieces of digital work. There are steps when it comes to wanting to learn the many ways or art, and I think the way I organized these categories made me visualize the process of a growth in someone who wants to start as a beginner, then try everything out there in the world of art.
