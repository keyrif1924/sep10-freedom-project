## Categorizing- By their purpose.

### Artistic preparation 
- PureRef
- XPPen drawing glove
- These technologies help artists prepare for and get comfortable with the artistic process.
- PureRef allows artists to collect, arrange, and organize reference images to help plan their artwork before creating it.
- The XPPen drawing glove reduces friction between the artist's hand and the drawing surface, helping make digital drawing more comfortable.
- These inventions focus on supporting artists before and during the creation process rather than directly immdeitaely going straight to work with struggles at they create their art pieces.

### Creative Assistance
- Xencelabs Quick Keys
- Tourbox lite
- Veikk VK1060 pro
- Drawing controllers and input devices
- These technologies provide additional controls and features that help artists interact with their digital art programs.
- Xencelabs Quick Keys and TourBox Lite provide customizable buttons and dials that allow artists to access shortcuts and adjust settings more easily.
- The Veikk drawing tablet provides a surface and digital pen for artists to control their digital drawings.
- These inventions help improve the drawing process by making digital tools easier to navigate and use. 

### Digital Creation
- Krita
- Rebelle
- Digital painting and illustration software.
- These programs provide artists with digital tools to create, edit, and experiment with different types of artworks.
- Krita offers customizable brushes, animation tools, and illustration features, while Rebelle stimulates traditional painting materials such as watercolor
- These programs allow artists to explore different artistic styles and techniques without always needing physical art supplies.
- They focus on directly on producing digital artwork and expanding the creative possibilities available to artists.

## Software
* 1st google research: "Art apps that artists can use"
  * 1st website looked into: <a href=https://krita.org/es/> Krita digital painting </a>
  * Krita is a free, open source digital painting and illustration program.
  * It offers customizable brushes for different art styles.
  * It includes brush stabilizers to help artists draw smoother lines vy reducing the effect of shaky hand movements
  * Its wrap around mode helps artists create repeating patterns and textures.
  * It also includes tools for 2D animation and comic creation.
  * Hardware: Windows, macOS, and Linux computers.
  * The cost: Free

* Second app I looked into: <a href=https://www.escapemotions.com/products/rebelle/about> Rebelle real media paint software</a>
  * Rebelle is a digital painting program designed to simulate traditional art materials.
  * It can imitate watercolor spreading, pigment blending, and other painting effects.
  * Artists can experiment with traditional looking paintings without needing physical paint.
  * It's unique for the way its painting engine stimulates, the way paint behaves helping digital artwork resemble traditional watercolor and other painting media.
  * Hardware: Supported Windows and macOS computers.
  * The cost: Rebelle 8 costs $89.99, while Rebelle 8 pro costs $149.99. Students and teachers can qualify for a 40% educational discount.

* Third app I looked into: <a href=https://www.pureref.com> PurRef</a>
  * Pureref is a visual reference board used by artists.
  * It lets artists collect and arrange reference images in one workspace.
  * Artists can use it to organize character designs, poses, and color inspiration.
  * It helps artists keep visual references nearby while creating art work.
  * It can be useful for planning illustrations before drawing.
  * It focuses on organizing visual references rather than creating artwork itself.
  * Hardware: Windows, macOs, and Linux computers 
  * The cost: Pay-what-you-want-model. You can choose how much to pay.
